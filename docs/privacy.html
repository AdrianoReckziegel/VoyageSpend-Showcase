Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026

Privacy First

1. Overview

VoyageSpend ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed with a strict privacy-first and offline-first philosophy. We believe your travel plans, financial budgets, and spending habits belong solely to you.

The App does not require an account, does not collect personal identifiers, and does not sell or share any of your data with third parties or advertisers.

2. Data Storage & Ownership

All data entered into VoyageSpend—including trip destinations, dates, budgets, categories, expense entries, notes, and currency preferences—is stored 100% locally on your device.

  • We do not operate backend servers that host or mirror your travel records.
  • You retain 100% ownership of your data at all times.
  • You can export your complete records as CSV or JSON backups whenever you choose.
  • Deleting a trip or uninstalling the app permanently removes all corresponding local data from your device.

3. Security & Biometrics

VoyageSpend provides optional security features to protect your data if someone else handles your phone:

  • PIN Protection: Your 4-digit security PIN is hashed using SHA-256 with a unique salt and stored in your device's hardware-backed secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). We never have access to your PIN.
  • Biometric Unlock: Fingerprint and Face Unlock use the official device operating system APIs. VoyageSpend never accesses, collects, or stores raw biometric measurements.

4. Network Permissions & Exchange Rates

The App requests standard Internet access solely for the following optional feature:

  • Live Exchange Rate Updates: VoyageSpend periodically fetches public foreign exchange rate pairs (e.g. USD to EUR, CAD to JPY) from public foreign exchange APIs so you can convert expenses on the go.
  • No personal information, device identifiers, or expense data is ever sent with these currency rate requests.

5. Analytics & Third-Party Tracking

VoyageSpend contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no behavioral tracking, and no marketing analytics. We do not track your location or profile your spending.

6. Children's Privacy

VoyageSpend does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
